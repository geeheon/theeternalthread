What this site is — and where to begin

Most Christians hear hundreds of sermons but could not explain how Romans 6, 7, and 8 connect — or why it matters that they do.

Scripture has a structure — a logic that runs from Genesis to Revelation. But that logic is almost never laid out as a whole. This site maps it. Each page is an interactive flowchart built around a passage or theological framework. Tap any card to expand the Scripture and explanation beneath it.

The material draws from writers like Watchman Nee, T. Austin-Sparks, DeVern Fromke, and Frank Viola, but the pages stand on the text itself. You do not need to know those names to follow the argument.
